# Brag Plan: Enkaji Enkitati CBO website

## What is this app?
Public website and admin CMS for a women-led community organization in Ewaso Ng'iro, Narok County, Kenya, with five programs, events, news, gallery, donate and partner pages.

## The angle
A calm, confident launch film in the organization's own words and brand colours. Every line is quoted from the site; nothing claims results.

## Hook (0-3.5s)
Eyebrow "Narok County, Kenya", then the headline "Empowering Women, Sustaining Futures" with a gold rule drawing under it.

## Key moments
- Logo mark with "A women-led community organization in Ewaso Ng'iro"
- The five program names arriving one by one on cream cards
- "Lasting change starts at the grassroots" with the site's own supporting line

## Outro / punchline
Logo, Donate (gold fill, green text) and Partner with us buttons, enkajienkitaticbo.org.

## User flow worth showing
none - landing-page only (the site's public home and program structure are shown).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, warm, grassroots dignity
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, restrained motion, no jokes.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.5s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #1C380E (forest green), #F7F1E1 (sand)
- Accent: #C4A050 (harvest gold), #EEE3C4 (bead cream)
- Text: #EEE3C4 on green, #1C380E on sand
- Display font: Libre Baskerville
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: cream logo mark on forest green

## Share copy (draft)
Empowering Women, Sustaining Futures: meet Enkaji Enkitati CBO, a women-led community organization in Narok County, Kenya.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (bundled), 120 BPM
- Music treatment: starts at 0, volume 0.45, 1s fade in, 1.5s fade out
- Music cue guidance: strongCue 16.02s locks the outro reveal; beat grid 7.02, 7.52, 8.02, 8.52, 9.02s for the program cards
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (extraction helper not available in this run)
- SFX posture: none (polished restraint)
- Restraint rule: no voice, no effects over the music

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 - Hook - 3.5s
Eyebrow and headline in cream on green, gold rule draws. Sequential: no. Transition: soft crossfade -> Scene 2
### Scene 2 - Who we are - 3.5s
Cream logo mark, "Enkaji Enkitati CBO", "A women-led community organization in Ewaso Ng'iro." Transition: soft crossfade -> Scene 3
### Scene 3 - Five programs - 6.3s
Five cards, one per beat from 7.02s, all held together from 9.5s. Transition: slide -> Scene 4
### Scene 4 - Grassroots - 3.6s
"Lasting change starts at the grassroots." plus supporting line. Transition: gold wipe on the 16.02s cue -> Scene 5
### Scene 5 - Outro - 4.5s
Logo, Donate and Partner with us, domain. Music fades out.

**Music mood:** upbeat, warm
**Audio summary:** a soft music bed that lifts at the outro cue and fades out.
