# Menstrual health: facts we teach
World Menstrual Hygiene Day is 28 May; 28/5 stands for a 28-day cycle and about 5 days of bleeding.

- Menstruation is a normal biological process, not a disease or a curse.
- Most girls start between 9 and 16 years old; every body is different.
- A cycle usually lasts 21 to 35 days; bleeding usually lasts 3 to 7 days.
- Good hygiene: change pads every 4 to 6 hours where possible, wash hands, bathe, wear clean underwear, dispose of pads properly.
- Period poverty is real: many girls miss school because they cannot afford pads.
- Boys and men should learn about menstruation too, so they can support girls respectfully.
- Seek medical help for very heavy bleeding, severe pain, unusual discharge or periods that stop unexpectedly.

| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| Menstruating girls are unclean | Menstruation is natural and healthy |
| Girls should hide during periods | Girls can continue normal activities |
| Talking about periods is shameful | Education helps reduce stigma |
| Only women should learn about menstruation | Boys and men should also understand and support |
