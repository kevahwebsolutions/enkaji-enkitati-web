## About (draft copy)
Enkaji Enkitati CBO is a women-led community-based organization in Narok County, Kenya. We work in rural Maasai communities around Ewaso Ng'iro to strengthen women's economic independence, keep girls in school with dignity, support vulnerable households and build skills across the community. We believe lasting change starts at the grassroots, where communities identify their own problems and lead their own solutions.

## Vision (draft)
A society where women and girls live with dignity, equality, and opportunity.

## Mission (draft)
To empower women and girls through education, health, economic empowerment, and advocacy for gender equality and sustainable community development.

## Core values (draft)
Integrity, Accountability, Inclusivity, Compassion, Empowerment, Service.

## Constitutional objectives
1. To empower women economically and socially
2. To promote sustainable livelihoods
3. To support vulnerable families in the community
4. To enhance education and skills development
5. To mobilize resources for community development projects
6. To promote unity and cooperation among members

## Our approach
Community-led change: community dialogue forums, school outreach, training and capacity building, advocacy campaigns, and partnerships with schools, county government, NGOs and the private sector.

## Governance and accountability
- Elected committee of five officials; membership open to adults who support the CBO's objectives, admitted on application and committee approval.
- Monthly general meetings; quorum of at least 50% of members; decisions by majority vote; minutes recorded by the Secretary.
- Funds come from membership fees, donations, grants, fundraising and income-generating activities, and are used transparently for CBO objectives.
- Bank withdrawals need two signatories, one of whom must be the Treasurer.
- Records kept: cashbook, member contributions register, bank statements, receipts and vouchers.
- Reporting: monthly financial report, quarterly activity report, annual general report, and donor reports when funding is received.
- Every project is approved at a general meeting; the committee oversees implementation and the Treasurer tracks expenditure.
- Constitution amendments need a two-thirds majority at a general meeting. On dissolution, assets pass to a similar community organization in Narok County.
