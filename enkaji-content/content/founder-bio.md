Caren Naeku Dapash is the founder and Chairperson of Enkaji Enkitati CBO. Born in Olokurto, Narok, she holds a BSc in Coastal and Marine Resource Management from Kenyatta University (Second Class Honours, Upper Division). She is an entrepreneur, youth mentor and community advocate who speaks on menstrual health, gender-based violence, ending FGM and environmental conservation. She speaks English, Kiswahili and Maa.
