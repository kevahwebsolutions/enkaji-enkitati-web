# How our table banking (VSLA) works
A Village Savings and Loan Association lets members save together, borrow from the group and share the profits.

- Open to CBO members who agree to the savings rules and attend meetings.
- Members save a fixed amount every week or month, recorded openly at each meeting.
- Members can borrow up to three times their savings, with committee approval, at an agreed interest rate and repayment period (1 to 6 months).
- Interest grows the group fund. At the end of each cycle (about 12 months) savings plus interest are shared out.
- A small emergency fund is kept aside for members in crisis.
- The Chairperson runs meetings, the Secretary records every transaction, the Treasurer handles cash, and two witnesses are present whenever money changes hands.
