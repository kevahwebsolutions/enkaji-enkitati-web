## The challenge
Women and youth in Ewaso Ng'iro face high unemployment and little access to credit or financial services. Without savings or capital, good business ideas stay small.

## What we do
- Village Savings and Loan Association (VSLA) / table banking groups
- Small business training: beadwork, tailoring, cosmetics, poultry farming
- Financial literacy training
- Startup support and a revolving fund for women's enterprises
- Market linkages for women's products

## Projects
- Women Micro-enterprise Fund
- Handicraft and beadwork cooperative
- Poultry and dairy goat project
- VSLA groups
