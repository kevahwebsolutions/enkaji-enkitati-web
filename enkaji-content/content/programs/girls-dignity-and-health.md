## The challenge
Many girls miss school during their periods because they cannot afford sanitary towels, and stigma keeps them silent. Harmful practices such as FGM, early marriage and gender-based violence still affect girls in our communities.

## What we do
- Menstrual health and hygiene education in schools, for girls and boys
- Sanitary towel drives and dignity packs (pads, soap, underwear, information leaflet)
- Mother-daughter conversations and boys' engagement sessions
- Anti-FGM community dialogues and GBV awareness campaigns
- Referral to health, legal and psychosocial support

## Key messages
- Periods are natural, not shameful.
- A pad can keep a girl in school.
- Menstrual health is a human right.
