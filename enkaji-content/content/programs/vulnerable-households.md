## The challenge
Widows, orphans, elderly people, persons with disabilities and very low-income families are often the first to fall through the cracks.

## What we do
- Monthly food baskets for vulnerable families
- School fee support for orphaned and needy children
- Emergency support during illness or disaster
- Elderly care and disability inclusion support
- Linking members to government funds (Uwezo Fund, Women Enterprise Fund, NGAAF)

## Projects
- Community relief food basket program
- School fee support fund
- Health support referral system
- Emergency hardship fund
