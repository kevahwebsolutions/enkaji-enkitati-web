## The challenge
Land degradation, water scarcity and limited activities for young people hold the community back.

## What we do
- Tree nursery and reforestation
- Rainwater harvesting and water storage tanks
- Community clean-up and sanitation drives
- Environmental awareness campaigns in schools
- Youth sports and talent development

## Projects
- Community water harvesting project
- Tree nursery and reforestation program
- Clean village initiative
- Youth sports empowerment league
