## The challenge
Many young people in rural Narok lack role models, career information and support to stay in school. Adults who missed school have few chances to learn.

## What we do
Our school mentorship program, **Ignite Dreams Initiative**, visits primary and secondary schools with talks, Q&A sessions and activities. We also run career guidance, book donation drives, girls' school-retention support, adult literacy classes and leadership training.

## Primary school topics
1. Dream Big Early
2. Importance of Education
3. Discipline and Good Character
4. Avoiding Bad Influence
5. Confidence and Self-Esteem
6. Leadership Starts Young
7. Cleanliness and Personal Hygiene
8. Respecting Parents and Teachers
9. The Power of Hard Work
10. Kindness and Unity
