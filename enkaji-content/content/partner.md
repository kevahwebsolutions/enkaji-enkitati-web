# Partner with us
We invite donors, NGOs, government agencies and businesses to support women and girls in rural Maasai communities.

Why partner: grassroots trust, SDG-aligned programs, a focus on women and girls, a community-led and transparent approach (two-signature banking, monthly financial reports).
